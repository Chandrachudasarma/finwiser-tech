Why this is hard

It takes reading every bank and card statement — thousands of noisy lines, self-transfers that double-count — to land on the one number that actually matters: what you can truly invest each month.

Design Rationale

Statements → one-number resolve

A dozen real, messy narrations across four banks and three cards sit as a faint ghost; the clean card eats them down to Income − Expenses = surplus. You see the volume that was consumed, not just the tidy answer.

"Real surplus, not a guess"

The hero number is what survives after rent, EMI, an existing SIP, and — critically — after stripping self-transfers that would otherwise double-count income. That's why it's the actual investable figure, the unlock for every later decision.
