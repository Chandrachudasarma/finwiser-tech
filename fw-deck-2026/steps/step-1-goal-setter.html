Why this is hard

People can't rank their own goals or put real dates and numbers on them — and the same money can't serve a 2-year and a 20-year goal at once.

Design Rationale

Chaos resolved into a ladder

Eight competing life goals with no order sit as a faint ghost cloud behind the answer — you feel them being consumed. In front, the same goals snap into a ranked, dated ladder. The frame shows the job already done, not the struggle in motion.

Priority + timeline is the foundation

Near horizons read warm (pink), far horizons read cool (blue) — a 2-year and a 25-year goal literally pull to opposite ends. Until goals are ranked and dated, no later step — net worth, surplus, or the action plan — has anything to optimise against.

Colour & state system

Green = a hard leg resolved (this node is done). Grey ghost = the un-ordered mess that was eaten. Pink/blue horizon tags mark the opposing time pulls. Identical tokens to every other relay node, so it lifts cleanly into the 4-up slide.
